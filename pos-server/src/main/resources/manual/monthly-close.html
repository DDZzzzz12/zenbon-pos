1. Purpose and Overview

The Monatsabschluss (monthly close) block is the tool for month-end reconciliation. It shows, for one period, whether all business activity is internally consistent, fiscally complete and ready for handoff to the accountant (DATEV).

Open the web back office, go to Kasse & Abschluss → Analysen and choose the period with the stepper at the top of the Berichte screen (Tag, Woche, Monat or Jahr, with back and forward arrows). The Monatsabschluss block below the sales figures follows the selected period and reloads whenever it changes.

The block is a report, not a workflow: nothing is stored when you open it, there is no “close month” button, and no row can be ticked off. Every status is recomputed on each request.

2. KPI Cards and Sparklines

At the top of the block, seven KPI cards summarise the closing figures of the period. Each card shows the value, a sparkline of the daily series, and a delta against the previous period of the same length.

CardWhat it shows
Bruttoumsatz (gross revenue)Revenue including VAT, derived from the bills (after promotions, without employee tips) — also for periods that are already closed. See 3.3 for why it can differ from the Z-Bon roll-up on pre-1.1.0 closings.
Nettoumsatz (net revenue)Gross minus VAT.
Mehrwertsteuer gesamt (VAT total)Sum of all VAT in the period.
Anzahl Belege (bills)Count of bills.
Rückerstattungen ohne Zahlartwechsel (refunds)Money actually handed back: Stornos without the Stornos of a Zahlartwechsel (Storno + Neubuchung), which refund nothing. It equals the Z-Bon roll-up’s Rückerstattungen minus its davon Zahlartwechsel.
Trinkgeld (tips)Tips recorded.
Kassenbestand am Ende (cash-book closing)Balance of the fiscal cash book (Ladenkasse) at the end of the period. Other books — the Tresor, other Nebenkassen — are not included; a balance always belongs to exactly one book.
All figures are shop-wide: they count every employee’s bills, whoever opens the block. Revenue, VAT, refunds and tips always cover the same set of bills, so net revenue can never come out negative because of a mismatch. Cash is recognised by the payment type’s “cash” flag, whatever it is called (e.g. BAR). The Kassenbuch roll-up lists the fiscal Ladenkasse’s entries only.

3. The Sections of the Block

Below the KPI cards the block shows four sections, in this order.

3.1 Checklist

The readiness checks described in Section 4, each with its status and, where there is something to say, a localized detail line (see 3.2). A check that does not apply to the shop’s configuration is not shown at all.

3.2 Discrepancies (Differenzen)

The checklist filtered to what is actually wrong. Each row can be expanded to a detail line and shows the number of affected records. The detail line is shown in the language of the back office (German, English or Chinese) — e.g. Kein DATEV-Export deckt den ganzen Zeitraum ab für: Kassenbuch or, for check 3, the VAT difference as amounts in euro. The server sends a code with the figures, not a sentence; only a back office older than the server shows its English fallback text. When nothing is wrong, the section says so explicitly — an empty Differenzen section is the answer you came for.

3.3 Z-Bon Roll-Up (Z-Bon-Zusammenfassung)

Every Z-Bon (end-of-day closing) in the period, with number, start, end, gross, net, VAT, tips, refunds (Rückerstattungen — the stored fiscal Storno total, as printed on the Z-Bon, Zahlartwechsel included), davon Zahlartwechsel and cancellations (Streichungen), plus a totals row. All figures are the closings’ stored values.

Reconciliation line. When the stored Z-Bon gross differs from the revenue the closings’ own bills give, a line under the table shows both, the difference and the affected Z-Bon numbers. The known cause is closings created before version 1.1.0: they stored the gross as Σ billed amount before promotions (e.g. a free item on a stamp card), while their net, VAT and payments exclude the promotion. Since 1.1.0 the gross is stored after promotions, so newer closings reconcile exactly. Stored Z-Bons are fiscal records and are never changed; the revenue cards follow the bills.

Above the table a timeline draws one bar per Z-Bon against the period and a full-height error bar for any gap between Z-Bons that contains bills. A gap without bills is not drawn — the shop was closed, which is not a finding.

3.4 Kassenbuch Roll-Up (Kassenbuch-Zusammenfassung)

The opening balance of the fiscal Ladenkasse, the period’s cash-book entries grouped by entry type with their subtotals, and the closing balance. All amounts are signed by the server; nothing is re-summed in the browser.

The VAT breakdown by rate and the payment-method breakdown are part of the server’s response but are not shown as separate sections in this block. Use the other panels of the Berichte screen or the Tagesbericht for them.

4. Month-End Checklist

Each check shows OK, Warning or FAILED, in this order:

#CheckWhat it verifiesIf not OK: where to look
1 Z-Bon-Kette intakt zbon_chain_integrity No two Z-Bons of the period overlap in time. Gaps between Z-Bons (closed days) are not flagged here. Section 3.3. An overlap points to a data-integrity problem; contact support.
2 Belege außerhalb Z-Bon no_bills_outside_zbon No bill falls into a gap between two Z-Bons. FAILED with the number of gaps that contain bills. Section 3.3 (red bar in the timeline). Bills outside every Z-Bon are missing from the daily closings and therefore from DSFinV-K and DATEV.
3 Mehrwertsteuer konsistent vat_consistent The VAT summed over the period’s bills matches the VAT total of the Z-Bon roll-up within €0.01. The comparison is on the total only, not per rate. Section 3.3. Check for bills changed or cancelled after their Z-Bon.
4 Keine offenen Belege no_open_bills No bill in the period is still unassigned to a Z-Bon (cancelled bills excepted). An open bill means the Z-Bon for that day has not been run. Run the Z-Bon at the till (Chapter 11).
5 Alle Belege TSE-signiert all_tse_signed No bill in the period has a TSE status of pending, processing, queued or failed. Cancelled bills and bills with TSE disabled are not counted. Section 3.2. Check the TSE status at the till (Settings → TSE) and the back office Geräte → TSE-Befunde.
6 Keine simulierten TSE-Signaturen no_simulated_signatures No bill, Kassenbuch entry, order or cancellation in the period is marked SIGNED while its TSE transaction carries the simulator’s serial number (SIM-TSE-001, in any stored encoding). Such a Vorgang has no valid TSE signature (§ 146a AO) although its status says it has — the state a NORMAL-configured shop without reachable TSE hardware produced before 2026-09-19. Check 5 looks at the status only and passes these rows; this check fails with the number of affected Vorgänge. Records from a configured SIMULATION setup (status SIMULATION) are not counted. Section 3.2. WORM data cannot be corrected: do not edit the rows. Document the affected period (e.g. an Eigenbeleg explaining it) and contact support — re-queuing the affected Vorgänge for Nachsignieren is a separate, owner-approved step.
7 Keine möglichen Doppelsignaturen no_possible_double_sign Reports Vorgänge for which the TSE response was lost after the request was sent and the signing queue re-signed the payload under a new transaction number. The device may already hold a signature for the original payload, so the WORM log can permanently carry two signatures for the one Vorgang. WARNING, not FAILED: nothing is provably wrong, only unprovable either way. Section 3.2. WORM data cannot be corrected: document the affected period (e.g. an Eigenbeleg) and contact support. Re-queuing for Nachsignieren is a separate, owner-approved step.
8 Alle TSE-Transaktionsnummern nachvollziehbar tse_tanr_continuity Per TSE (by its hex serial), lists transaction numbers the POS has no ordinary record of — gaps in the device’s numbering and numbers recorded twice (e.g. after a device was stored under two serial encodings) — that no entry in the TSE explanation register currently covers. WARNING, not FAILED: BSI TR-03153-1 §9.4 makes a repeated call, and therefore a fresh transaction number, the prescribed reaction to a failed or unanswered TSE call, so a gap is explainable rather than wrong in itself. The check only reports what nobody has explained yet. Back office → Geräte → TSE-Befunde. Open the finding and add an explanation, or verify it is already covered by an existing register entry.
9 Alle Bargeldbewegungen dokumentiert all_cash_documented Every cash-book entry of the period has a linked document (Beleg). FAILED with the number of entries without one. Attach the missing Beleg at the till (Finanzdokumente → entry → Beleg hinzufügen), see Chapter 12.
10 DATEV exportiert datev_exported An automatic DATEV export covers the period: in the Monat view, one export covering the whole month; in the Woche view, every ISO week of the period. A Kassenbuch export is always required; an AP-ledger (Eingangsrechnungen) export only when its automatic cadence is switched on and the period holds at least one creditor invoice. Ad-hoc exports (source Manuell) do not satisfy this check. Section 5. With the automatic export switched off, this check cannot pass; that alone does not make the month financially wrong.
Tip: Checks 1–6 and 9 are data-integrity checks. Checks 7–8 are TSE advisory checks. Check 10 is a reminder about the handoff. Passing checks 1–9 with an empty Differenzen section means the period is financially sound regardless of whether the DATEV export has run.

5. Automatic DATEV Export and its Cadence

The automatic DATEV export is configured in the back office under Compliance → Datenexport, section DATEV XML Konfiguration → Automatischer Export. It has a master switch (Aktiviert), a run hour, and a separate cadence per export type:

SettingOptions
Kassenbuch-FrequenzAus · Wöchentlich · Monatlich
Kreditorenbuch-Frequenz (AP ledger)Aus · Wöchentlich · Monatlich
Buchungsstapel-FrequenzAus · Wöchentlich · Monatlich

The export runs as a scheduled job (visible under System → Geplante Aufgaben). A type set to Aus is skipped. The Buchungsstapel export additionally requires a saved DATEV account mapping; without one it is refused (see Chapter 12, DATEV-Export).

6. How the Automatic Export Catches Up

Each run inspects the export log per type and exports every complete period (ISO week or calendar month, per the type’s cadence) that has no automatic log row yet, working forward from the last one. The current, incomplete week or month is always skipped.

  • Each exported period produces its own log row (source Automatisch) and its own file on the server.
  • Files are written to the directory configured as Server-Exportpfad in the DATEV XML configuration, in a subfolder named after the period (e.g. 2026-05 for a month or 2026-W23 for an ISO week).
  • Use Herunterladen on a log row under Compliance → Datenexport → DATEV-Export to retrieve a copy.

If a period fails (for example because the file cannot be written), the run stops for that type and leaves the later periods for the next run. Exporting a later period first would advance the log past the failed one, and it would never be caught up. The scheduled job is then recorded as Fehlgeschlagen, listing each failed and each deferred period, and the Automatischer Export panel shows that status — not Erfolgreich. Check 10 stays FAILED until the failed period has been exported. See Geplante Aufgaben.

The export log is append-only: prior rows are never modified. Exporting does not lock a period or prevent further business activity.

7. Ad-Hoc Export

For a one-off extract — e.g. re-sending a period to the accountant — use the buttons in the DATEV-Export section under Compliance → Datenexport: Kassenbuch (DATEV-XML), Eingangsrechnungen (DATEV-XML), Buchungsstapel (CSV), Buchungsstapel + Belege (ZIP), Alle drei exportieren and Ausgangsrechnungen (DATEV Debitoren).

  1. Click the button for the export you need. The Ad-hoc-Export dialog opens.
  2. Choose Startdatum and Enddatum and confirm with Exportieren.
  3. The file is downloaded by your browser.

Ad-hoc exports are logged. Each ad-hoc export writes a row to the DATEV export log with source Manuell. Manual rows are shown in the log but are ignored by the automatic catch-up and by checklist item 10, so an ad-hoc export never shifts the automatic export history.

If the Buchungsstapel export carries notices (e.g. receipts dated into an earlier period or an earlier fiscal year), they are shown above the export list after the download.

8. Weekly Close (Week View)

Businesses that reconcile weekly select Woche in the period stepper of the Berichte screen. The block then covers one ISO week (Monday 00:00 to the following Monday 00:00), and checklist item 10 is evaluated per ISO week instead of per month.

Weeks are numbered according to ISO 8601: the week containing the first Thursday of the year is week 1. In folder names they appear as YYYY-Wnn, e.g. 2026-W23.

For weekly DATEV files, set the relevant cadences in Section 5 to Wöchentlich; the automatic export then writes each week to its own folder, for example:

<exportPath>/2026-W23/…

9. Closed Days and Z-Bon Gaps

Not every calendar day has business activity. The block handles this correctly:

  • A gap between two Z-Bons without bills is a closed period. It produces no finding and is not drawn in the timeline.
  • A gap that contains bills is a genuine problem: checklist item 2 shows FAILED and the timeline draws a red bar.
  • Bills after the last Z-Bon that have not been closed yet show up in checklist item 4 until the Z-Bon is run.

A Z-Bon covers the time from the previous closing to its own, not a calendar day, so a closing after midnight needs no adjustment. For planned closures of several days (e.g. a summer break), no configuration is needed: simply run a Z-Bon on the first trading day.
